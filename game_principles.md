Theme: Dark fantasy fortress / base-building simulation

all artwork is tiles of 32x32 pixels with 256 color palette, total colors across the whole app! so 256 strict palette rather than each artwork having its own 256 colors

Dark/night/easy on eyes UI and artwork

Player: 300 year old necromancer who just wants to be left alone

Threat: Humans see you as unnatural and repeatedly attack your fortress

View/style: Top-down, retro pixel 8-bit graphics, chunky but with visual clarity; rooms are built as procedural multi-tile rooms that can be stamped down; can do individual tiles for things like walls and moats and traps;

Simulation: Real-time with pause, meant to be watched like an ant farm as systems run in addition to being actively played

Core focus: Base building, fortress design, chokepoints, walls, traps, towers, rooms, etc.

Progression: The fortress gets stronger, not the player character

Player character: Exists and can die and that's the game loss condition; stays physically squishy with no stat upgrading

Workers: Raise skeletons to automate hauling, building, gathering, crafting

No pawn babysitting: Skeletons have no food, mood, romance, sleep, or social needs

Automation: Increasingly automate fortress labor and logistics

Enemy variety: Human melee troops, archers, mages, battering rams, sappers, siege equipment

Scaling: More "stuff"/wealth in fortress means stronger and more varied human attacks as in RimWorld; but also scales slowly with time as in Factorio so you can't just check out mentally

Design goal: sandbox and emergent gameplay, without RimWorld-style arbitrary grind or micromanagement

Scope philosophy: Small enough for mobile, works well with mobile controls, but gets its depth through interacting systems and emergent game systems
